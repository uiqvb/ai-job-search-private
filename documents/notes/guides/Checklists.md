# Application Checklists

Both official DeakinTALENT checklists, in tick-off form. Run these before every submission.
Sources: `Source/Resume-Guide-v6.pdf` pp.11-12, `Source/Cover-Letter-Guide.pdf` pp.15-16.

---

## Resume checklist

### Personal details

- [ ] First name and surname in font size 20-24 (preferred name in brackets if relevant)
- [ ] Professional email address
- [ ] Phone number formatted `xxxx xxx xxx`
- [ ] Residential address (optional)
- [ ] Customised LinkedIn URL (optional)
- [ ] Link to portfolio or website (if relevant)
- [ ] **NOT included:** photo, age, date of birth, family details, gender, marital status,
      nationality, religion, or the words "CV" or "Resume"

### Opening statement

- [ ] Heading is "Career Profile", "Profile", or "Professional Summary"
- [ ] Three to six lines, paragraph format
- [ ] Neutral language, no personal pronouns
- [ ] Tailored to the role and industry, relevant key skills and experiences only

### Technical skills summary (optional)

- [ ] Included if relevant to the role and industry, in concise bullet format
- [ ] **NOT included:** soft skills such as "strong communicator" or "highly motivated"

### Education

- [ ] Heading is "Education"
- [ ] Reverse chronological order
- [ ] Start and end dates, month and year
- [ ] Full correct qualification title including specialisation or major
- [ ] Full correct name of the university or institution
- [ ] Location (suburb if Australian, city and country if international)
- [ ] Noteworthy achievements below in concise bullets: scholarships, academic awards, WAM,
      exchanges or tours, leadership roles, student societies

### Experience

- [ ] Heading is "Experience", "Professional Experience" or "Work Experience". Extra headings if
      useful: "Relevant Industry Experience", "Internships", "Projects", "Voluntary Experience"
- [ ] Reverse chronological order
- [ ] Start and end dates, month and year, for every position
- [ ] Full correct organisation name
- [ ] Full correct position title
- [ ] Location (suburb if Australian, city and country if international)
- [ ] Neutral language, no personal pronouns
- [ ] Every bullet follows **action verb + problem/situation + result/outcome**, focused on
      skills and results rather than tasks
- [ ] Bullets tailored to the role and ordered by relevance

### Additional information

- [ ] Extra headings considered where relevant: Professional Membership, Courses and
      Certifications, Volunteer Work, Projects, Extra-Curricular Activities, Languages, Interests

### Referees (optional)

- [ ] Heading is "Referees"
- [ ] Omitted entirely, or "Available upon request", or two to three referees with name, role,
      organisation and relationship. Email and phone optional

### Overall impact

- [ ] Spelling and grammar accurate, capitalisation correct, UK English throughout
- [ ] Understandable on fast review, short sentences, plain English
- [ ] Abbreviations and acronyms only where necessary, defined on first use
- [ ] **Length does not exceed three pages**
- [ ] Bullet points used, no lengthy paragraphs
- [ ] **NOT included:** graphics, tables, photos, borders, colour, icons, banners
- [ ] Balanced content, no excessive white space and no cramming
- [ ] Consistent font, Calibri or Arial, 12-14pt headings and 10-11pt body
- [ ] Consistent highlighting (capitalisation, bold, italics, underline)
- [ ] Consistent spacing and alignment (margins, section spacing, headings, dates)
- [ ] Consistent bullet style and alignment
- [ ] Professional file name, `First name Surname Resume.pdf`

## Cover letter checklist

### Resources

- [ ] The DeakinTALENT Cover Letter Template was used as the base, saved and renamed rather than
      rebuilt from scratch

### Contact information

- [ ] Personal contact details as per the template
- [ ] Email address is professional and checked regularly
- [ ] Mobile has voicemail set up with a clear professional message
- [ ] Recipient's contact details included as per the template
- [ ] Date included
- [ ] Greeting line: "Dear Firstname Lastname", or "Dear Recruitment Manager" / "Dear HR Manager"
- [ ] Attention line included, with job reference if relevant
- [ ] **NOT included:** photo, date of birth, marital status, religion, nationality, or the words
      "Cover letter"

### Introduction

- [ ] Brief greeting, e.g. "I am writing to apply for the position of..."
- [ ] Full correct position name and full correct organisation name
- [ ] States where the opening was found (Deakin Jobs and Internships Board, Seek, LinkedIn)
- [ ] Specifies any referral, meaning a direct connection to a current or former employee
- [ ] One or two lines on why you are interested, at a high level

### Body (one to three paragraphs)

- [ ] Describes specifically why you are a suitable candidate, emphasising what you **offer**
      rather than what you gain
- [ ] Describes two to three relevant qualifications, personal skills, technical skills,
      experiences or achievements, drawn from paid work, internships, volunteering or
      extra-curriculars
- [ ] Every skill statement is evidenced with a specific example, result or outcome
- [ ] Describes specifically why you are interested in the organisation, showing real research

### Quality of content

- [ ] Tailored to the specific role and organisation, aligned to the qualifications, experience
      and skills in the position description
- [ ] Incorporates keywords from the position description, job listing and organisation website,
      and uses industry-specific language
- [ ] Adds context and depth to the resume rather than duplicating it
- [ ] **NOT included:** "willing to do anything", "open to all opportunities" or similar

### Overall impact

- [ ] Spelling, grammar and capitalisation accurate, UK English
- [ ] Easily read on fast review, short sentences, plain English
- [ ] Tone positive, approachable and professional. **NOT included:** "I hereby apply for",
      "Moreover, I", "My aforementioned skills", "This fine establishment"
- [ ] Buzzwords not overused ("expert", "passionate", "dynamic"). "Best candidate" and
      "perfect fit" avoided
- [ ] Abbreviations and acronyms only where necessary, defined on first use
- [ ] Numbers one to ten written as words
- [ ] **Length does not exceed one page**
- [ ] Font style and size match the resume (Calibri or Arial, 10 or 11pt), single spaced, left
      justified
- [ ] Formatting matches the resume (margins, bullet style) so both read as one package
- [ ] **NOT included:** icons, photos, headings, banners, borders, colour, tables, lists
- [ ] Professional file name, `First name Surname Cover Letter.pdf`
