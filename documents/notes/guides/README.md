## The five rules most likely to sink an application

1. **One page for the cover letter, three pages maximum for the resume.**
2. **No personal pronouns in the resume.** They belong in the cover letter and on LinkedIn only.
3. **Follow the application instructions exactly.** Not doing so can mean immediate rejection.
4. **Match their words.** If the ad says "highly motivated", the application must demonstrate
   sustained motivation with a specific example, in those terms.
5. **Bullets are accomplishments, not duties.** Action verb, then problem, then result.
