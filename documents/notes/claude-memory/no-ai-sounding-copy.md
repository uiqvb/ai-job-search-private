Write portfolio, letter and email copy the way a real student writes: plain, specific, a bit of dry humour from true details. Never riff on the employer's own taglines (e.g. "The intelligence layer for one small cardboard robot"), never quote their values back as "Your words: ..." labels, and avoid stock AI devices: "Plot twist", "Not a few. Zero.", "Receipts first.", "Two confident liars", colon reveals, triplet slogans, cute labels like "Candidate file"/"Asset record", jokey captions like "not data from a real candidate".

**Why:** Manit, 2026-10-22, on the Syncrowin portfolio: "reads like AI so strongly... Copying their company lines to fit my thing. Very shit... Dont make it so AI-ey."

**How to apply:** Show values through what he did, in his own words, and mention the company's ideas in a sentence of paraphrase at most. Keep the design styling; it was the copy he disliked. Related: [[video-script-style]]
