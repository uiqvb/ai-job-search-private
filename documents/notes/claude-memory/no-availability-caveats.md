Don't put visa work-hour limits, part-time fallbacks or date-overlap caveats in a CV or cover letter. State only when he can start (e.g. "available to start on 18 January 2027"). Never claim full-time for a period he can't work, either.

**Why:** Manit (2026-10-21, Infosys InStep): volunteering the caveat "gives them a reason to reject". He'd rather raise it if they ask or once there's an offer.

**How to apply:** Still flag timing overlaps to Manit in the fit evaluation and the tracker notes, just keep them out of the documents. Related: [[year-level-third-year]]
