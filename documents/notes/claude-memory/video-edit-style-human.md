Full research + rules: documents/video/EDITING-STYLE.md. Edit intro videos in an old-PewDiePie, human-made style: hard cuts and snap zooms that follow his head, whole frame going black-and-white for a beat, echo on punchline words, objects thrown off-screen with the camera following, then a hard cut. Text = plain white with black outline (meme style), no coloured boxes, no bounce. Hats drawn in code in plain colours, pinned to the face track (photo-cutout hats were rejected in Draft 2). Captions throughout: white, black outline, 2-4 words, the spoken word turns yellow. Punchline beats (B&W/echo, explosion) about 0.7 s. Music for Syncrowin being chosen after Draft 3.

**Why:** On Syncrowin Draft 1 (22 Oct 2026) he said the grey chase tunnel, cracked-screen glitch, lime company palette, vector hats and fake typing terminal looked "hella AI-ey"; he wants it to feel like a person edited it.

**How to apply:** Default to this for every intro video edit; glitch only applies to him (cutout), not the whole screen; the Claude Code reveal is a typing-terminal animation (he asked for it in Draft 3 after the real screenshot fell flat), showing only steps that really happened.
