Manit believes intro videos work well as a way in at small, funded startups that need people (first one: Andromeda, sent 18 Oct 2026). From 20 Oct 2026, the plan is a video for Tier 1 targets in the startup EOI list: one core cut plus a 10-15s opening recorded for each company.

**Why:** small startups rarely post internships; a video stands out from a cold email.
**How to apply:** use only YouTube Audio Library music (a Pixabay track got blocked); test the unlisted link in a private window before sending (the Andromeda email first went out without the link).
