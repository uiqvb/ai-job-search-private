# 15. The Club Involvement Track Record

**Hook:** The supporting evidence paragraph. Not a story, a stock of facts to draw two or three
from.

**Used in:** Deakin SEA, Deakin Assistant, Suncorp. Usually as the second-last body paragraph.

**Best for:** filling the "two to three relevant experiences" requirement in
`../Guides/Cover-Letter-Guide.md` once the main story has done the emotional work.

## The facts available

**Deakin Data Science Club, LinkedIn Manager, January 2026 to present**

- Own the club's public channel end to end: content direction, design, scheduling, performance
  measurement. Consistently 1,000+ impressions per post.
- The LinkedIn strategy was mine, not inherited or executed on someone else's brief.
- Hashtag and tagging strategy, format and timing testing against LinkedIn analytics.
- Built an alumni outreach and re-engagement pipeline, cold-contacting alumni, speakers and
  sponsors.
- **Hosted and anchored networking sessions** connecting students with alumni and industry
  guests. Anchoring is DDSC only.
- Coordinated with sponsors on co-branded content, managed the club inbox.
- Designed event graphics and promotional creative.

**Deakin Software Engineering Club, Committee Member, July 2025 to April 2026**

- Built the React application behind the debugging workshop as part of the dev team, and solely
  designed the seeded defects. Around 30 attendees. See `04-designing-the-defects.md`.
- Floated the workshop as a helper.
- Fronted the Market Day stall. See `06-market-day-hesitation.md`.
- Weekly committee planning, proposed an open-source development program and a club hackathon.
- Event photography.
- DSEC has 200+ members overall.

**Deakin Future Students, Student Ambassador (Open Day)**

- **Selected.** This is the newest and most directly relevant credential for any Deakin-facing
  application, and it is the role the Student Experience Assistant position leads.
- The Assistant letter uses it well: "By the time this contract starts I will have worked the
  shift myself, and I would rather lead a team having stood where they are standing."

**Boroondara Stroke Support Group, IT Support (volunteer), July 2025 to present**

- Maintain the WordPress site as part of a small volunteer team.
- Ran the monthly activity-calendar publication cycle across 12 months, then trained new
  volunteers to run it themselves.
- Diagnosed and rolled back a change that broke a live page, restoring service without external
  developer support.
- Authored a redevelopment plan backed by a Site Health audit, which identified the hosting
  tier's memory limit as the blocker.

**7-Eleven, Customer Service Assistant, July 2025 to October 2025**

- Solo shifts, opening and closing unsupervised, cash and EFTPOS reconciliation including
  variance investigation, trained new starters, escalated complaint handling.
- Used in letters as the source of patience and composure with someone who is confused,
  frustrated, or unsure what to ask.

students at Market Day activities that contributed to more than 100 new member sign-ups. I have
hosted networking sessions, supported students through a 30-person debugging workshop and

stories 02 and 01. "These experiences have started showing me what changes when someone else is
actually relying on the work you do."

## Rules for using this file

- **Pick two or three. Never list all of it.** The guide asks for two to three experiences
  described in detail, not a catalogue.
- Each one needs a result attached, per the APR formula in `../Guides/Resume-Guide.md`.
- ⚠️ **The 100+ sign-ups figure must appear once per document.** It currently sits in both the
  DSEC and DDSC resume entries and the README flags this as unresolved.

## Open questions carried from the README

- Is the 100+ Market Day figure DDSC, DSEC, or both combined?
- Were the open-source program or hackathon proposals ever adopted or run? If yes, that is a far
  stronger bullet than "proposed".
- How many alumni contacted, and how many networking sessions run?
- LinkedIn posting cadence.

## Do not say

- "Taught" or "delivered" the workshop.
- Anchoring at DSEC. That was DDSC only.
- Follower growth figures. Dropped at Manit's instruction, do not re-ask.
- Any 7-Eleven commendation, and do not use 7-Eleven as a referee.
