## How this reads, for the record

Three emails, one LinkedIn message and a handful of connection notes is a normal amount of
interest at a company whose values include "uncomfortable levels of curiosity". It is
one-directional, which is expected from a student. It stays on the right side because each
message is specific, offers something back (the demo), and asks for no favour. It would turn
bad through chasing, adding more people, or sending near-identical messages.
