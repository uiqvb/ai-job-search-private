# ReSource Pty Ltd - Engineering Intern, Multi-camera Imaging and Identification Station

Saved 15 October 2026. Founder Troy Rowe (Deakin alum) accepted Manit's connection request the same day.
Apply: CV + short practical project example or portfolio to careers@re-source.au. State what you personally built, whether hardware or software is your stronger area, availability, and whether applying with a partner.

---

Build a system that photographs, identifies and records real IT equipment, then help put it to work on the factory floor.

ReSource Pty Ltd is recruiting two engineering interns to develop an operational multi-camera imaging and identification station. We recover, test and remarket used IT equipment through our IT asset disposition (ITAD) operation. Your work will help our team capture better images and asset data with less manual processing.

## What You Will Build
ReSource plans to supply a working proof of concept using a Sony A7 III camera and version 1 software. You will build on it, with freedom to improve the hardware and software within the budget and agreed maintainability requirements.

- The physical station: camera selection and positioning, lighting, fixtures and a practical operator workspace.
- The vision pipeline: device-focused images, background removal or cropping, serial-number and product-label crops, barcode reading and optical character recognition (OCR).
- The integrated workflow: authorised access to RazorERP (our asset-recording system), accurate asset matching, automatic posting of validated, high-confidence items, and operator review of exceptions.
- The operational handover: tested hardware and software, clear error recovery, source code, build and maintenance documentation, and operator training.

Scope: laptops, desktops, servers, phones/tablets, network equipment and components (HDDs, SSDs, RAM, CPUs, motherboards, GPUs, PSUs). Representative test items and acceptance criteria agreed early.

Design target: under 30 seconds per device from capture trigger through processing and RazorERP upload, excluding operator handling. A target to validate, not a reason to trade away accuracy or reliability. ReSource retains safety and production-release approval.

## What You Need
Final-year or Masters students and recent graduates with complementary strengths. Backgrounds: robotics/mechatronics, mechanical, electrical or software engineering, computer science. Across the pair: practical hardware/prototyping capability and strong Python, computer-vision and API integration skills. "Show us what you have built, how you tested it and what you learned when it did not work." Apply individually or as an existing pair.

## Training and conditions
ITAD workflow, the supplied proof of concept, RazorERP requirements, site safety. Two scheduled technical sessions per week, support from the founder and internal technical team.
- Up to A$15,000 hardware budget, separate from wages.
- Keilor East ITAD factory, primarily on-site, flexible hours, occasional remote software work by agreement.
- Portfolio evidence: approved demo, photos and technical case study.
- Possible further paid work or graduate role.
- 12 weeks full-time within November-February, allowing for the Christmas shutdown.
