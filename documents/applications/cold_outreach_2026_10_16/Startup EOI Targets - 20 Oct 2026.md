## Intro video plan
- Video goes to Tier 1 only (16 companies). Tier 2 gets email + resume + portfolio.
- Keep one core cut (~60s: who you are, robot demo, Open Day rollback, what you want). Record a new 10-15s opening per company naming their product and why you want in. That makes 16 videos doable.
- Upload unlisted to YouTube. Put the link in the first two lines of the email. Use a thumbnail image (robot on screen) linked to the video, not an attachment.
- Music: YouTube Audio Library only (the Pixabay track got blocked).
- **Before every send:** open the link in a private window, read the whole email once, check every question is complete. (The Carina email went out without the link.)
- Send in batches of 4-5 a day so each one gets tailored.
