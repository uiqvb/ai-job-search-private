# Industrial Product Engineering Intern, Megapack, Energy - Tesla

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4482848068 (fetched 2026-10-23 via linkedin-search)
Location: Melbourne, Victoria, Australia

What To Expect
Tesla’s Industrial Energy Product Engineering team is responsible for diagnosing, analysing, and resolving issues within our utility-scale battery energy storage products. As an Intern, you will engage in software-driven problem-solving and project-focused tasks that boost the performance, reliability, scalability, and commissioning of Tesla’s energy storage solutions. You will help develop analytical tools, workflows, and data-driven insights that optimise fleet operations and improve the customer experience. By creating analytics that surface performance, operational, and maintenance trends, you will help drive decisions that keep our growing global fleet operating at its best.

This is a 6-month internship opportunity, with the potential to extend or convert to full-time. The successful candidate must be able to work a minimum of 3 days/week from our office.

What You'll Do

Collaborate with Field Service and Customer Support teams to troubleshoot and resolve hardware, software, and firmware issues that cause downtime for Tesla’s Industrial Storage customers.
Proactively identify and investigate complex electrical, network, and software problems, partnering with Design, Software, and Quality teams on root cause analysis to drive long-term product improvements.
Serve as a product expert for new site deployments—testing, diagnosing, and resolving issues to ensure seamless, on-time commissioning.
Assess hardware and software design changes, conducting in-market or regional testing as needed.
Build software tools that streamline commissioning tasks, minimize escalations, and proactively flag field issues.
Use Python (or similar) and large-scale data platforms to develop analytics and diagnostic tools, revealing performance and reliability trends across Tesla’s global fleet.

What You'll Bring

Completed or currently pursuing a degree in Electronic, Electrical, Renewable Energy Systems, Photovoltaic, Mechatronics Engineering or a related field
Solid engineering fundamentals and intuition, especially for firmware/software-enabled systems
Understanding of power systems and electronics
Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment, maintaining a proactive, solution-focused attitude when faced with very challenging problems

Desired Competencies:
Experience with battery systems and power systems
Familiarity with SQL, Python, or comparable tools for large-scale data analysis
Knowledge of CAN Bus communication
Experience with developing, managing or maintaining equipment running Linux based-applications
