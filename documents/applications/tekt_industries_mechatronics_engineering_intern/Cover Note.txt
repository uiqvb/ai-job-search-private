I'm Manit, a Software Engineering (Honours) student at Deakin University, entering my penultimate year in 2027. I'd love to join your Concept Development team as a Mechatronics Engineering Intern.

The thing I've built that I'm proudest of is a two-wheel robot for my Embedded Systems unit. I eventually traced it to voltage sag at the buck converter, using the robot's own boot and reset logs, and fixed the power supply instead of rewriting software that was never the problem. I then built a .NET backend that controls the same robot over Wi-Fi, which scored 100/100 and was demonstrated at Deakin Open Day.
