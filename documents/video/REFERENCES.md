## Collections (Manit, 22 Oct 2026: "gold mine")
- **PremiumBeat, "Video Resumes - A Refreshing Alternative to Demo Reels"** (Danny Greer, 2014):
  https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/video-resumes-a-refreshing-alternative-to-demo-reels/
  Sorted into "About Me" and "Hire Me" styles. Picks most relevant to Manit:
  - Nick Belling (nickbelling.com) - **software developer**; projects + academics, overlays linking to CV/portfolio. Closest to Manit's profile.
  - Matthew Epstein, "Google Please Hire Me" (googlepleasehire.me) - funny, high-quality plea to one company; Google didn't hire him, startup SigFig did (biteable/peoplehum).
  - Aaron Williams (aaronwilliams.tv) - a **60-second** pitch, made in the software the job used. Same idea as Manit's "made with AI" reveal.
  - Antonin Waterkeyn (antoninwaterkeyn.com) - motion graphics with edgy humour.
  - Jonas Mosesson (vimeo.com/jonasmosesson) - made for one firm (Buck); Stephen Niebauer (sniebauer.com) - pitch to Vimeo that worked.
  - Workopolis "Video Resume: What NOT To Do" - parody of bad video CVs (what to avoid).
- **Hongkiat, "13 Brilliant Video CVs"**: https://hongkiat.com/blog/job-application-videos
- **Biteable, video resume examples**: https://biteable.com/blog/video-resume-examples/
- **Wyzowl, 13 best animated resumes** (motion-graphics style): https://www.wyzowl.com/animated-resume/
- **Coburg Banks, 10 funny video CVs**: https://www.coburgbanks.co.uk/blog/10-super-duper-funny-video-cvs
- **Cornerstone, best and worst moments in video resumes** (pitfalls: too sarcastic, too long): https://www.cornerstoneondemand.com/resources/article/lessons-best-and-worst-moments-video-resum-s/
- Also mentioned: Graeme Anthony's interactive YouTube video CV (2011, got hired); Robby Leonardi's playable game resume (not a video, but the same "show, don't tell" idea).
