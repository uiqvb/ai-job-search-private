## Filming notes
- Phone on a tripod or propped, same spot for every take. Plain wall behind you, good light from the
  front (makes the props, hats and fire sit cleanly on you).
- Palm moments (0:03 and 0:41): same hand, same height, held still for 2-3 s each.
- Toss at 0:11: from palm out, flick the hand back over the same shoulder, then freeze with a straight face for 2 s; the chase is spliced in between.
- Hats at 0:28: head fairly still so the hats sit right.
- Film each row separately, 2-3 takes; talk fast, the edit adds the pauses.

## How it gets made (be ready to say this truthfully)
- Editing: Claude Code + the Remotion editor (same pipeline as Capgemini): cuts, captions, timer,
  pop-ups, hats, flings, transitions.
- Fling cutaway: built entirely in Remotion (grey space, zoom past the shoulder, speed lines, spinning items); only the explosion is a clip. Trash can = an image (AI-generated or illustrated).
- Fire and explosion: must be made with an AI video tool so "made with AI" holds.
- Sound effects: free SFX library (whoosh, pop, boom, glitch, clapper, ding).
- Risk: at ~150 words it runs about 60-65 s. If over, trim row 0:20 first.

## Camera setup and acting guide (v7, simplified)

**Setup:** phone vertical at eye level, about 1.2 m away, waist up, space above your head. Face a
window. Stand on the same spot every take.

**The whole video: just talk to the camera like it's a friend.** Only 4 special moves:
1. **Palm out** (reel at 0:03, fire at 0:41): hand out to the side, palm up, chest height. Hold still.
2. **Toss over the shoulder** (0:11, on "that"): from the palm-out position, swing the hand back
   over the same shoulder, like flicking something behind you (hand ends near your shoulder).
   Keep looking at the camera, straight face, hold 2 s.
3. **Point** at the camera on "this whole video" (0:35).
4. **Close your fist** on "Imagine that for Syncrowin" (0:47), then wave at the end.

Everything else (hats, words, timer, glitches, explosion) is added in the edit. Keep your head
fairly still while talking about the hats.

**Filming method (Manit, 22 Oct): one long continuous take**, not separate clips, so his position
never changes. Script taped just under the phone. For each line: read it, look back at the lens,
wait 1 s, say it. Messed up? Pause, say it again. Never move your feet. The editor cuts from the
first word to the last word of the best take (same as Capgemini), so the glances and silences
disappear.

**Cut note (Manit, 22 Oct, after filming):** for row 0:35 use the take where he says "using Claude
Code with AI" (word order flipped). The correctly worded take has mic problems. Still accurate.
