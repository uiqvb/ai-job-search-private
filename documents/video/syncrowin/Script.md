**How to film it (natural, not read):** learn the beats, not the words. Say each section in your own
words, two or three takes, keep the one that sounds like you talking to a friend. Small stumbles are
fine. Only lock the hook, "But you don't want to hear about that", and the close.

## FILMING VERSION (v1, SUPERSEDED by v3 below, ~280 words, about 1:50)

**1. Hook** (to camera, fast, upbeat music)
"Okay, Syncrowin. One minute. Here's why you should hire me."

**2. The reel** (jump cuts, stat pops up on screen each line)
"I got a hundred out of a hundred in Backend Development. My robot got picked for Deakin Open Day.
And I made a robot control API six times faster, with five thousand robots running."

**3. Pace shift** (music cuts out, lean in, slower)
"But you don't want to hear about that. You're a startup. You want someone who's curious, who
experiments, and who has the energy to keep trying things. And that's pretty much how I work."

**4. The curiosity story**
"Most of what I build starts with a 'what if'. Earlier this year I had two separate uni projects:
an API for controlling robots, and a robot I'd built myself. Nobody asked them to work together.
But what if my API could drive my robot? So I tried it. For about a month, it didn't work. Then it
did."

**5. The Syncrowin link**
"That same robot taught me something you'd recognise. It kept resetting at random. So instead of
guessing, I went back through the data it was sending, and found a power converter that was
failing. That's a tiny version of what Syncrowin does for whole plants."

**6. Many hats**
"And at a startup, you don't get to be just one thing. I've been the backend guy, the hardware guy,
and the IT volunteer for a stroke support group. And today, I'm the video guy."

**7. The AI reveal** (to camera, a bit cheeky)
"Because this whole video? I made it with AI. Claude Code helped me cut my takes, time the captions
and line up every clip. And it can do things a phone camera can't. Like this."
[Palm out to the side, facing up. Beat. Fire appears in the hand. Hold, look at it, look back.]

**8. Why Syncrowin**
"You've just raised three million dollars, so a lot of people are about to want to hear your story.
I'd love to help build the product, and help you show it off too."

**9. Close** (to camera, warm)
"So yeah, I'm Manit Khera. Give me a 'what if', and I'll go find out. Thanks for watching."
Hold two seconds for the end card.
