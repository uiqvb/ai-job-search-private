## FILMING VERSION (v4, ~245 words). Speak it like you're telling a friend.

**1. Hook** (to camera) *(new)*
"Have you ever been told you can't do something, and then done it anyway?"

**2. Who I am** (to camera) *(same as v3)*
"Hi, I'm Manit. I'm in my third year of Software Engineering at Deakin, and I'd love to join
Capgemini this summer as an IT Analyst intern."

**3. The setup** (next to the robot) *(same as v3)*
"This year, I had two final projects in two different units. One was a backend server, and the other
was a small robot. I wanted the server to actually drive the robot. When I told my unit chair, he was
honest with me: if I tried to join them, I might not finish either one properly. I did it anyway."

**4. The "what if"** (next to the robot, slower) *(new)*
"Getting the robot to move wasn't the hard part. The hard part was asking, what if? What if the robot
loses Wi-Fi halfway through a job? To handle that, every command I send carries its own undo. If the
robot hears nothing for seven seconds, it uses those undos to back itself out, step by step. And when
the robot reports what it did, the server replays it in a simulation before recording it."

**5. How it turned out** (to camera, small smile) *(same as v3)*
"All that checking paid off. The project scored a hundred out of a hundred, and I got to show it to
students and parents at Deakin Open Day."

**6. Why Capgemini** (to camera) *(new)*
"Capgemini describes boldness as taking considered risks and showing commitment, and joining those two
projects was exactly that for me. I also read your report on agentic AI, which found trust in fully
autonomous AI agents fell from 43 to 27 percent in a year. Asking 'what if' before letting a system
act on its own is how I like to build, and I'd love to learn how you do that with real clients."

**7. Close** (to camera, smile, energy back up) *(new)*
"So yeah, I'm Manit Khera. I build things people tell me I can't. Thanks for watching."
Hold two seconds for the end card.
