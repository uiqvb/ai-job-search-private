# Affinda intro video: storyboard (v3, DRAFT for Manit)

For the Affinda Internship Program, 18 Jan - 26 Feb 2027 (Cremorne). Application drafted 27 Oct 2026,
closes 20 Nov 2026. The video link goes with the online application (or a follow-up email).

**Theme (Manit, 27 Oct): proof of the three things the ad asks for: great ideas, curiosity, energy.**
Two-minute pitch: achievements and builds fast, then the resume goes in the bin ("a resume can't show
you the good stuff"), then one beat each: great ideas = making the two unit projects meet; curious =
the "what if" and the unit chair's warning; energy = this video, made the day he found the ad, edited
with Claude Code. Then what he's working on now (Prompt Wrappers, Capsule), why Affinda, close.
Same energy as Syncrowin.
**Frame device: a 2:00 countdown** (Syncrowin's timer, doubled). Plain white monospace numbers
top-right, slam in on "two minutes" and tick down all video. He finishes early: the timer freezes on
whatever's left and "WITH TIME TO SPARE" flashes.
**Running gag: the resume.** Achievements pop off it, he bins it (bin explodes, smokes in the corner
all video), and it flies back out and unfolds as the end card.
**Checklist:** after the bin, three empty boxes slam in down the side: `GREAT IDEAS` `CURIOUS`
`ENERGY`. Each gets a hand-drawn red tick at the end of its row. On screen only; he never recites the
ad's words.
Edit style: old-PewDiePie, human-made (`../EDITING-STYLE.md`): a hard cut or snap zoom on every line,
B&W + echo on the one deadpan beat, meme text, karaoke captions throughout. No Affinda logo or
colours. Music: `pixel_peeker_polka_faster.mp3` (the Syncrowin track) from the first word.

## Pace (Manit: lots of energy, fast-paced, like Syncrowin)

- Every line is its own cut. No breath gaps left in: the cutter trims to the first and last word.
- A snap zoom or hard cut-in at least every 3-4 s, alternating tight and wide so it never sits still.
- The reel (0:08) speeds up: each pop lands a little sooner than the last.
- Only two held beats: the straight face after the toss (0:21) and the B&W stare (0:33).
- Talk faster than feels normal; the edit can slow a beat, it can't add pace.

## Filming

Same setup as Syncrowin: phone vertical at eye level, ~1.2 m, waist up, face a window, plain wall,
room above your head and to your side (for the toss). One long take from `Filming Sheet.txt`: read a
point, look at the lens, wait 1 s, say it. Mess up? Pause and say the whole point again. Don't move
your feet.
**Prop:** one printed copy of your resume (it gets crumpled). Print a spare for a second toss take.
Moves: big energy (0:00), resume up (0:04), tap it per item (0:08), crumple + toss, freeze 2 s (0:21),
fists out → smash (0:25), shrug / straight face / wince (0:33), grin (0:41), point at the lens
(0:45), flick a switch (0:53), fingers walk (1:04), point at the paper stack (1:14), wave (1:20).
