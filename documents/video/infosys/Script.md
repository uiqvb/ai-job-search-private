## FILMING VERSION (v4, ~265 words, about 1:50)

**1. Hook** (to camera)
"For a whole month, my robot ignored everything my API told it."

**2. Who I am** (to camera)
"Hi, I'm Manit. I'm in my third year of Software Engineering at Deakin, and I'd love to join InStep
this January."

**3. The setup**
"That month started with two final projects. One was a backend API for controlling robots. The
other was a robot I built myself. Nothing in either brief said they should meet, but I wanted that
API to actually drive that robot."

**4. The hard part** (slower)
"My unit chair backed the idea, but he was honest. It was ambitious, and I might end up finishing
neither project properly. For weeks, it looked like he was right. Sometimes the API worked and the
robot just ignored it. Sometimes the robot moved, but the API thought something completely
different had happened."

**5. How it turned out**
"Eventually, the API spoke to the robot, and the robot listened. That project scored 100 out of
100, and it was picked from my cohort for Deakin Open Day. So I got to stand there and show future
students and their parents my robot, running live, driven by my own API."

**6. Why Infosys**
"Going past what's expected is the part I enjoy most, and it's why MatchFeel at the Australian Open
stuck with me. Most people would never expect to follow a tennis match by touch. But with MatchFeel, a
magnetic ring traces the ball across the court, so blind and visually impaired fans can. That's what
going beyond expectations looks like, and it's the kind of work I want to do at InStep."

**7. Close** (to camera, warm)
"So yeah, I'm Manit Khera. I usually end up doing more than the brief asks. Thanks for watching."
