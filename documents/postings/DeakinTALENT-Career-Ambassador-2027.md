# DeakinTALENT Career Ambassador

**Employer:** DeakinTALENT (Deakin's Careers and Employment Service)
**Program:** Employer Roles Recruited by DeakinTALENT / Student Jobs at Deakin
**Employment type:** Casual, temporary contract
**Location:** Burwood
**Pay:** HEW 1, $40.55 per hour
**Hours:** approximately 4 hours per week, Monday to Friday, on campus
**Apply with:** updated resume + short application form. **No cover letter required.**
**Ad saved:** 16 September 2026

## About the role

Year-round paid opportunity with flexible hours to accommodate study and other
responsibilities. Career Ambassadors help create meaningful and practical career
experiences for fellow students: peer-to-peer support, helping students with resumes,
supporting LinkedIn headshots and career activities, contributing to events and other
student engagement initiatives. Ambassadors also take part in professional development
and experiences through the year to build their own skills and employability.

## Key responsibilities

- Providing peer-to-peer support to students by answering questions about DeakinTALENT's
  career programs and events and connecting students with relevant career resources and
  services.
- Supporting the delivery of practical and meaningful career experiences for students,
  such as resume reviews, LinkedIn headshots, career activities and other student
  engagement initiatives.
- Supporting the DeakinTALENT team at a range of events and activities, including
  Orientation, Club Days, career fairs and other on-campus initiatives.
- Contributing to social media content and promotional campaigns that help engage
  students with DeakinTALENT.
- Helping create welcoming and engaging DeakinTALENT spaces and activations for students
  seeking career support.
- Participating in staff meetings, training and professional development opportunities.
- Providing feedback, ideas and suggestions to continually improve the student experience
  and effectiveness of Career Ambassador activities.

## About you and eligibility

- Current Deakin student, having completed a minimum of 1 trimester and with a minimum of
  **three trimesters still to complete**.
- Interest in supporting other students and connecting them with useful career
  experiences and opportunities.
- **Strong communication skills**, confidently engaging with people from a range of
  backgrounds.
- Undertake professional development and upskilling training during the trimester break.
- Compulsory Deakin training and a **valid employee Working with Children Check**.
- Available approximately **4 hours per week, Monday to Friday, on campus**.
- DeakinTALENT is broadening the range of students it engages, so it encourages all study
  areas with **particular focus on all Arts degrees, all Science and LES courses, and
  Psychology**.
- **Must be available for the compulsory Group Assessment Centre: Thursday 29 October,
  Burwood.** Only apply if that date works.

## Benefits

Paid casual position at HEW 1; flexible on-campus work around study; supportive Career
Ambassador cohort building connections, community and belonging; professional development
and upskilling through the year; end-of-year celebration and award ceremony.

## Process and dates

1. Application reviewed by the DeakinTALENT Employer Services Team.
2. Video interview if progressing.
3. Final shortlist notification.
4. **Group Assessment Centre and selection session, Thursday 29 October, Burwood**,
   during the Inter Trimester Break / T3 O Week. Attendance is mandatory to remain
   eligible.
5. **2 to 6 November:** offers and unsuccessful applicant communications.
6. **Mid November onwards:** onboarding ahead of 2027 induction.
7. **Early 2027:** induction.

Detailed feedback is provided to applicants unsuccessful after the final shortlist or the
Group Assessment Centre.

## Eligibility note

All candidates with work rights considered, **including international students**.
