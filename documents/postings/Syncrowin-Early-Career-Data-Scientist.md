## About
Apply data, maths and ML to real problems in energy, manufacturing, mining and critical infrastructure, with experienced AI engineers, data scientists and domain experts, across the full ML lifecycle. "We value demonstrated ability, initiative, and a willingness to learn over years of experience."

## Responsibilities
- Clean, analyse and interpret large industrial and operational datasets
- Build and evaluate statistical and ML models for forecasting, anomaly detection and optimisation
- Exploratory analysis, experiment design, communicate findings
- Preprocessing, feature engineering and evaluation pipelines
- Time-series and sensor data from industrial settings
- Work with AI engineers on accuracy and robustness; research and prototyping
- Document methods, experiments and model performance

## Requirements
- Bachelor's/Master's in data science, CS, maths, statistics, engineering, physics or related
- 0-3 years incl. internships, academic research or personal projects
- Strong Python
- Statistics, probability, linear algebra, ML fundamentals
- SQL
- Structure analytical problems; communicate findings clearly
- Detail, curiosity, initiative

## Nice to have
Forecasting / deep learning / optimisation; PyTorch or TensorFlow; Azure or other cloud; sensor, IoT, energy or industrial data; research, Kaggle or open source; MLOps, deployment, data engineering; mathematical modelling / OR.

## Offer
Competitive pay + ESOP; mentorship from AI engineers and technical founders; full AI lifecycle; small flat team, room to own things.
